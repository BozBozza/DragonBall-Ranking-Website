Kilis To Power Levels

To be able to calculate most of the power levels in Dragon Ball Z I used the 2nd power system introduced in the Buu Saga, that being Kilis. We get 3 confirmed Kili counts in the anime, those being:
- Super Saiyan Goku: 3,000 (This will later be proved to not be accurate, but it is still useful here)
- Yakon: 800
- Dabura: 4,000

If we believe that Power Levels and Kilis are linear then we can use the canonical Super Saiyan multiplier of 50 to work out that Base Goku has a Kili count of 60. Which doesn't make sense since we see Base Goku beating Yakon before they are sent to his homeworld (Power Levels should not be affected by your environment). A better estimate for Goku's Kili count in Base would be 1,000 as it is still stronger than Yakon (also it is a nice number), this means that in Kilis the Super Saiyan multiplier is 3x Base. This means that the relationship between Kilis and Power Levels is exponential and not linear. We can show this relationship and work it out using logarithms:
3x = 50
log(50) / log(3) = 3.560876795...

This means that we can convert any Kili count into a power level. I have done it with the confirmed ones:
- Super Saiyan Goku: 2.6 Trillion (This is not used as Goku was massively supressing his power)
- Yakon: 23 Billion
- Dabura: 6.7 Trillion

This is the basis for all the other power levels on this list.

Super Saiyan Blue and Super Saiyan 4 Multipliers

The reason I have grouped the multipliers of these two transformations together is because they both share a lot of similarities with the main one I want to focus on is how they are portrayed in other dragon ball media. They are often portrayed as equals in stuff like Dragon Ball Heroes and the video games, I am going to be using this interpretation despite Dragon Ball Super probably being a lot higher than Dragon Ball GT. We also know that Super Saiyan Blue is also called Super Saiyan God Super Saiyan meaning it is a 50x boost ontop of Super Saiyan God
Since they are equal and we know the multipliers for the transformations before it we can model it as:
400 (SSJ3) x SSJG x 50 (SSJB) = 500 (Golden Great Ape) x SSJ4

We also know that from our Fusion analysis the multiplier for Fusion, we can use this since Super Saiyan God is stated to be stronger than Vegito, we can model it like this:
400 x 3 x 50 < 400 x SSJG

This means that the multiplier for Super Saiyan God must be above 150x Super Saiyan 3.

Looking at the Dragon Ball GT side it is actually wrong, since we know that Baby Vegeta had transformations which mimic the Super Saiyan forms we can just use the same multipliers there. The problem comes where base Baby Vegeta (Super Saiyan equivalent) is already stronger than Super Saiyan 3 Goku, and we put Goku's Golden Great Ape at 500x:
Let's say Base Baby Vegeta is B and Base Goku is G are equal for this model (they aren't really but they are close)
50B = 400G this means B = 8G
400B < 500G (what it is if Golden Great Ape is a 500x Multiplier) which means 3,200G < 500G which is wrong

The only way to make it work is to make the multiplier for Golden Great Ape be greater than 3,200x

We can now update our original equation to match our new findings about Super Saiyan God and Golden Great Ape:
400 (SSJ3) x >150 (SSJG) x 50 (SSJB) = >3,200 (GGA) x SSJ4

Since we need the greater thans to be significantly greater than as with both they are stated to be / are much stronger. As an estimate I would put Super Saiyan God at 200x Super Saiyan 3 and Golden Great Ape at 4,000x, this would make Super Saiyan 4 1,000x stronger than Golden Great Ape and put both Super Saiyan 4 and Super Saiyan Blue at a multiplier of 4,000,000x base.
These two transformations are extremely importatn for working out every other character's power level in their respective series.

Buu

Buu gains power through absorbing others and adding their power level to his own.
Kid Buu was created by Bibidi and first absorbs South Supreme Kai and becomes Buff Buu, gaining significant power. Kid Buu then absorbs Grand Supreme Kai but seemingly becomes weaker as later on Goku and Vegeta exclaim that Buu got stronger when Fat Buu was taken out of him and he becomes Buff Buu again. This implies that Grand Supreme Kai was limiting Buu's power rather than increasing it. Much later in the Buu Saga, the evil essence within Buu is released and is shown to be much stronger than Fat Buu. Then, Evil Buu absorbs Fat Buu to become Super Buu and seemingly gets stronger. This can be explained as Grand Supreme Kai's limitations on Buu becomes weaker as the more evil part takes control. For the rest of Buu's transformations, the power levels of the people he absorbed are added on to his own.

Super Buu is weaker than Kid Buu. This is because when Gotenks defuses within Buu and Buu becomes Buuccolo, Goku in Super Saiyan 3 states "you are no match for me now". Since Piccolo's power level was so insignificant at that point, Super Buu and Buuccolo were basically equal. Since Goku in Super Saiyan 3 was losing to Kid Buu, this shows that Super Buu is weaker than Kid Buu.
